Key insights

  1. Sep–Nov brings ~40% of sales; November alone is ~16%.
  2. Revenue grew 3% in 2011 through bigger baskets (+7%), while orders fell 3.7%.
  3. Top 20% of customers drive 77% of revenue; the top 1% drive 31%.
  4. Only ~20% of new customers return in month 1. Sep–Nov new customers fell 36%, partly a data-start effect.
  5. Ireland earns £623K from just 3 customers: a concentration risk.

Recommendations

  1. Prepare stock and campaigns from August, not November.
  2. Win back the 826 “At Risk” customers (£1.4M past revenue).
  3. Add a second-order incentive to lift month-1 retention; track acquisition over a longer history.
  4. Bundle the strongest pairs (paper cups + plates, Red Retrospot bowl + cup, napkins + plates).
  5. Reduce dependence on a few large accounts; grow Germany and France.
